On February 24, 2026, Dr. Cofresi presented **“One Episode of Visually Signaled Sips of Alcohol Changes Human Brain Responses to the Visual Signal”** at the Gordon Research Conference on *Alcohol and the Nervous System: Behavioral, Circuit, Cell, and Molecular Mechanisms of Alcohol* in Ventura, California. It was a fantastic opportunity to reconnect with colleagues who do preclinical (animal model) alcohol and addiction research, given that Roberto conducted preclinical research in undergraduate and graduate school.
