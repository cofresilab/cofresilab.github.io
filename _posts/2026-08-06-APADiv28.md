On August 6, 2026, Dr. Roberto Cofresí presented “Three-Headed Hydra: Incentive Salience-Based Risk Pathways to Substance Use” at the 2026 Convention of the American Psychological Association (APA) in Washington, DC.

The talk was delivered at the Division 28 (Psychopharmacology & Substance Use) Business Meeting as the Early Career Award Address, following Dr. Cofresí’s receipt of the Division 28 Early Career Psychopharmacologist Award.

The presentation highlighted his research on incentive salience and the different pathways through which it may contribute to risk for substance use.
