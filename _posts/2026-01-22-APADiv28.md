Dr. Cofresí has been selected as this year’s recipient of the Division 28 **Early Career Psychopharmacologist Award**. Division 28 of the American Psychological Association (APA) is the **Society for Psychopharmacology and Substance Use**. The Early Career Psychopharmacologist Award honors a young scientist doing original, meritorious work in psychopharmacology and encourages excellence in research at the interface between the disciplines of pharmacology and psychology. Congratulations!!


<a href="/2026/08/06/APADiv28.html"> Update August 2026: Dr. Cofresí received the award at the 2026 APA Convention in Washington DC from the President of Division 28, Dr. Cassandra Gipson-Reichardt.</a>
